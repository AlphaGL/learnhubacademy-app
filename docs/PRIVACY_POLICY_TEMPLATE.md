## Who we are
LearnHub ("we", "us") provides an educational mobile application for students.
Contact: **[support email]**.

## Information we collect
- **Account information** you provide: name, email address, and (optionally)
  phone number, created through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
- **Learning activity**: courses/materials you view and features you use, to
  power your dashboard and improve the app (via Firebase Analytics).
- **Device & technical data**: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud
  Messaging), device type, and crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics).
- We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or SMS.

## How we use information
- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To show your courses, materials, and personalised dashboard.
- To send notifications you opt into (exam reminders, new materials, results).
- To diagnose crashes and understand aggregate usage to improve the app.

## How information is shared
- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- We use trusted processors that handle data on our behalf:
  - **Supabase** (authentication and database hosting).
  - **Google Firebase** (push notifications, analytics, crash reporting).
- We may disclose information if required by law.

## Data retention & security
- Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- We retain your account data until you delete your account.

## Your rights & account deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any
time by **[in-app: Profile → Delete account]** or by emailing
**[support email]**. We will delete your account data within **[30] days**,
except where retention is required by law.

## Children
LearnHub is intended for users aged **[13]** and older. We do not knowingly
collect data from children under this age.
